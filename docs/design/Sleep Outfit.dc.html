Prototype · how it decides

Bedtime outfit picker

1. Overnight room temp. The room drifts toward the average outside temperature between 7pm and 7am (hourly forecast), but the radiator thermostat stops it falling below its setting. Humidity above 65% counts as 0.1–0.2 TOG warmer. Once you add the morning reading, the night’s temp becomes the average of bedtime and morning.
2. Guide TOG. A standard room-temp to total-TOG chart (14°C → 3.5, 20°C → 2.0, 26°C → 0.5).
3. Personal offset. Each rated night says what would have been right (worn TOG minus 0.3 per step warm). Recent nights count more.
Door & health. An open door assumes the room runs 0.3° cooler; once 3+ door-open nights are rated, it learns from those alone. Fever lowers the target 0.5 TOG. Teething, unwell and fever nights count at 20% weight when learning.
Naps learn separately once 2 are rated; until then they borrow the night offset.
4. Combo search. Every mix of available base layer, sleepwear, bag and socks is scored on distance to target, with sleeve rules below 19°C and above 23°C.
Sample wardrobe and six nights are pre-loaded. Data saves on this device. The outside figure is the 7pm–7am hourly Met Office forecast (UKV model, served via Open-Meteo) for your postcode (looked up via postcodes.io), or set it by hand. The 7am reminder fires while the app is open; an installed version would push it with the app closed.
